# Tinat Academy Resource and Curriculum Report

**Verification date:** 26 November 2026<br>
**Scope:** Authoritative, publicly verifiable resources for 18 medical/public-health research topics, with Ethiopian and African context.

## 1. Executive summary

Tinat Academy should be a practice-first learning environment, not a digital textbook shelf. The strongest launch base is:

- **Method core:** WHO health research methodology and TDR implementation-research materials.
- **Epidemiology/statistics core:** CDC Field Epidemiology Manual, CDC Principles of Epidemiology, OpenIntro/OpenStax, NCBI Bookshelf, R for Data Science, and Epi Info/jamovi/JASP.
- **Evidence and reporting core:** Cochrane Handbook, GRADE, EQUATOR, CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, CASP, and PROSPERO.
- **Research ethics core:** Declaration of Helsinki, CIOMS, Belmont Report, WHO ethics guidance, COPE, and Ethiopian institutional/national ethics procedures.
- **Practice core:** DHS data, DHS manuals, KoboToolbox/ODK, OpenEpi, G*Power, EpiTools, PubMed, Zotero, Rayyan, and OSF.

The academy's first release should cover one or two beginner modules, then a proposal module that forces learners to use Tinat's Sample Size Calculator, Statistical Calculator, Research Workspace, data collection tools, and literature features.

Verification caveat: links ending at organization or repository home pages are not treated as verified direct resource links. Several institutional sites block automated checks or use dynamic pages, so their status is stated explicitly rather than overstated.

## 7. Proposed Tinat Academy curriculum

### Module 1: Foundations of health research
- **Objectives:** Define health research, evidence, populations, exposure, and outcome.
- **Lessons:** Why research matters; ethical stewardship; evidence hierarchies; local/global health priorities.
- **Primary text:** Bowing or WHO methodology guide.
- **Assignments:** Critique a community health question.
- **Assessment:** 10-item concept quiz and one-page research-need statement.

### Module 2: Research problem, question, and hypothesis
- **Objectives:** Use PICO/PECO and PICo where appropriate.
- **Lessons:** Problem statement; feasibility; descriptive/analytical questions; hypotheses.
- **Assignments:** Convert five weak questions into answerable questions.
- **Assessment:** Peer rubric and automated objective scoring.

### Module 3: Objectives and protocols
- **Objectives:** Write SMART-aligned general/specific objectives and a protocol outline.
- **Assignments:** Create objectives and align them with outcome variables.
- **Assessment:** Protocol logic check.

### Module 4: Literature searching and evidence synthesis
- **Objectives:** Search PubMed, read abstracts, manage references, avoid selection bias.
- **Lessons:** Boolean search; MeSH; de-duplication; Zotero; screening.
- **Assignments:** Build a search string and a 20-record Zotero library.

### Module 5: Quantitative research designs
- **Objectives:** Select and defend descriptive, cross-sectional, cohort, case-control, and trial designs.

### Module 8: Questionnaire and instrument development
- **Objectives:** Develop valid questions and response options.

### Module 9: Data collection and management
- **Objectives:** Plan field workflow, data dictionaries, consent, privacy, quality checks.

### Module 10: Epidemiology
- **Objectives:** Measure prevalence, incidence, risk, odds ratios, confounding, and bias.
- **Lessons:** Epidemic curves, stratification, disease surveillance.

### Module 11: Biostatistics
- **Objectives:** Describe data, test hypotheses, estimate confidence intervals, interpret regression.

- **Assessment:** Statistical analysis report with a table and assumptions check.

### Module 12: Qualitative data analysis
- **Objectives:** Use codebooks, themes, reflexivity, and reporting.

### Module 13: Research ethics and informed consent

## 9. Interactive learning and Tinat integration

### Comparative sample-size lab
- Students enter assumptions in Tinat's Sample Size Calculator.
- Compare with OpenEpi and G*Power.
- Display formula assumptions, margin of error, cluster effect, attrition, and limitations.
- Unit tests should fail if Tinat and a trusted reference differ beyond an agreed tolerance for a fixed scenario.

### Ethiopia DHS statistics lab
- Use public DHS model or de-identified indicator data.
- Exercise: define a population; calculate prevalence and confidence interval; disaggregate; identify denominator and weighting issues.
- Export a reproducible Tinat/ R or jamovi workflow.

### Electronic questionnaire lab
- Use a KoboToolbox/ODK-compatible form design.
- Exercise: skip logic, required fields, ranges, unit coding, versioning, offline collection.
- Tinat Research Workspace should generate a data dictionary and a Kobo-ready schema where feasible.

### Critical-appraisal lab
- Randomize learners to observational, randomized, qualitative, or systematic-review articles.
- Require checklist completion and a 200-word evidence interpretation.
- Store responses and provide rubric feedback.

### Proposal workspace
- Guided proposal sections write directly into Tinat's Research Workspace.
- Link objectives to variables, sample size to outcomes, tools to data elements, and reporting checklist to study design.
- Add ethics approval status and version history.

### Assessment model
- 10-minute pre/post diagnostics per module.
- 5-application-item quizzes rather than recall-only questions.
- Scenario-based feedback.
- Completion certificates only after a passing capstone.
