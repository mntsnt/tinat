The Platform Architecture Slide 03 of 09
Core Innovation

Two complementary data collection pathways.

Digital Surveys

Self-Administered Online Studies

Designed for urban cohorts, university students, clinical practitioners, and smartphone users nationwide.

  • • Tinat Credits (TC): Transparent rewards automatically calculated from study length
  • • Zero Publisher Fees: Zero deposit required for researchers to gather public cohorts
  • • Targeted Filtering: Demographic filtering by age, gender, and regional location
Outcome: Rapid data collection within 48–72 hours
Ground Field Operations

Community Field Enumerators

Trained local enumerators conduct face-to-face interviews in clinics, health posts, and rural communities.

  • • Direct Birr (ETB) Payouts: Enumerators receive verifiable cash compensation
  • • Offline PWA Sync: Field collection functions completely without network connectivity
  • • Verbal Consent Protocol: Compliant informed consent recorded via voice & touch
Outcome: 100% representative geographic sampling in rural woredas
Dual Data Pathways Slide 04 of 09
Trust & Governance

Medical-grade ethics, de-identification & national trust.

01

Fayda National ID

Cryptographic verification via Ethiopia's National ID authority prevents duplicate accounts, sybil attacks, and bot responses.

02

IRB Ethics Gating

Mandatory Institutional Review Board (IRB) ethics clearance upload and admin verification before any study can activate.

03

Identity Separation

Participant identities and responses reside in cryptographically isolated database domains. Researchers never see citizen PII.

04

Small-Cell Suppression

Automated differential privacy engine suppresses demographic cross-tab cells below 5 respondents to eliminate re-identification.

Export Ready: Direct de-identified CSV and coded Excel outputs compatible with SPSS, Stata, R, and Python.
WHO & HIPAA Compliant
